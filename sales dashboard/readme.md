# 📊 Sales Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Dashboard** designed to provide insights into sales performance, profitability, and regional trends. The dashboard helps stakeholders quickly analyze key business metrics and make data-driven decisions.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **KPI Highlights**

  * Average Profit: 28.66
  * Total Quantity Sold: 38K
  * Total Sales: 2.30M
  * Discount Distribution by Region (Central, East, South, West)

* **Sales & Profit Analysis**

  * Comparison of **Average Profit** and **Total Sales** across customer segments:

    * Home Office
    * Corporate
    * Consumer

* **Regional Insights**

  * Breakdown of **discounts by category and region**
  * Regional performance comparison for better strategic planning

* **Trend Visualization**

  * Yearly and quarterly trends showing:

    * Count of sub-categories
    * Average sales performance over time

* **Detailed Sub-Category Table**

  * Profit and quantity analysis for product sub-categories such as:

    * Accessories
    * Appliances
    * Chairs
    * Phones
    * and more

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Data Visualization Tool (e.g., Power BI / Tableau / similar)
* Data Modeling & Aggregation
* Interactive Charts & Tables

## 📈 Use Cases

* Business performance monitoring
* Sales strategy optimization
* Regional and category-level analysis
* Executive reporting

## 📌 Summary

This dashboard consolidates complex sales data into an intuitive and visually engaging interface, enabling users to uncover trends, identify opportunities, and improve overall business performance.
